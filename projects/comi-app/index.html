Comi App
UI/UX & Branding — 2026

Comi is a swipe-style roommate matching app built for universities, with a commercial version designed to scale beyond the campus. Finding a roommate today is a patchwork of group chats, DMs, and half-filled matching forms — a high-stakes decision made with almost no real information. Comi turns that into something familiar: a swipe-forward interface that makes compatibility quick to read, with the questions that actually predict who you can live with surfacing underneath. The brand sits in the tension between cool and trust — casual enough that students actually want to use it, credible enough that schools and parents take it seriously.
